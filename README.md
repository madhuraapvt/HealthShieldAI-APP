# 🛡️ HealthShield AI

**Your Health, Your Shield**

HealthShield AI is an AI-powered healthcare mobile application designed to make medical information more accessible, secure, and convenient. It helps users manage digital health records, access essential medical information during emergencies, and explore healthcare services.

## ✨ Features

- 🚨 **Emergency QR Access** – Quick access to essential medical information during emergencies.
- 🏥 **Digital Health Profiles** – Manage important health information and emergency contacts.
- 📄 **Medical Report Management** – Organize medical reports and prescriptions.
- 💊 **Medication Reminders** – Get reminders for medications.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Services** – Access doctor-related features and healthcare support.
- 🤖 **AI Health Assistant** – Explore general health information with AI assistance.
- 🔐 **Security and Privacy** – Support safer access to sensitive medical information.

## 🛠️ Technologies

- Frontend: Flutter and Dart
- Backend: Python and FastAPI
- Database: Configurable database integration

## 🎯 Objective

To develop a user-friendly digital healthcare platform that simplifies health record management and improves access to important medical information.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

HealthShield AI provides health information support and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency services.
